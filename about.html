About Gastón Pillet

Product Designer (UX/UI) with 7+ years of experience leading the end-to-end design lifecycle for complex SaaS and B2B/B2C platforms and a background as a web developer. Expertise includes strategic design and AI-driven ideation in global remote environments.

My design process starts with the discovery phase, where I leverage AI to quickly analyze user research and data. I then use Lovable to quickly turn initial concepts into professional prototypes. I work closely with Claude Code and Copilot to ensure the design can be quickly turned into functional code. This full-cycle approach helps me make decisions better and guarantees precise, enterprise-level design outputs.
