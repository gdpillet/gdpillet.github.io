About Gastón Pillet

Product Designer (UX/UI) with 8+ years of experience leading the end-to-end design lifecycle for complex SaaS and B2B/B2C platforms and a strong background as a Web Developer. Expertise includes strategic design and AI-driven ideation in global remote environments.

Because I understand frontend architecture (HTML, CSS, JS), I naturally bridge the gap between Design and Engineering. I design with scalability in mind and speak the developers' language, ensuring smooth hand-offs and technically viable solutions.

I am passionate about accelerating the design process without compromising quality. Today, I actively integrate AI tools (Figma MCP, VS code, Claude) into my workflow to build rapid, high-fidelity functional prototypes. This allows my teams to validate ideas faster and iterate based on real data rather than assumptions.

I’m currently based in Málaga, Spain, working remotely with teams across the globe. Always open to talking about UX strategy, design systems, or the future of AI in design.
