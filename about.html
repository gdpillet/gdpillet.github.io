About Gastón Pillet

Product Designer (UX/UI) with 7+ years of experience leading the end-to-end design lifecycle for complex SaaS and B2B/B2C platforms and a background as a web developer. Expertise includes strategic design and AI-driven ideation in global remote environments.


I have recently focused on integrating AI tools into my UX/UI design workflow. For the last few months, I have been actively exploring and applying tools like Gemini to generate placeholder content and synthetic data, Claude to assist with functional code snippets, Lovable to transform concepts into high-fidelity prototypes, Figma Make for rapid visual ideation, and Dovetail for AI-assisted qualitative data analysis. This approach allows me to accelerate ideation, enhance decision-making, and ensure more precise, enterprise-level design deliveries.
