CASE STUDY 02

Scaled Ibancar's loan origination by 3.5x while achieving profitability 6 months ahead of plan


Role

Lead UX/UI Designer

Team

3 Developers, Marketing, CEO

Impact

3.5x Loan origination was successfully scaled over the past 12 months, 6 Months Profitability was achieved ahead of schedule, 2.2% Credit losses were maintained at an exceptionally low rate of revenue.

Tools

Figma, Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, Useberry, Jira

About Ibancar

Ibancar is a car-secured loan platform serving clients across Spain. The company connects investors with borrowers, lending capital with interest while minimizing risk through rigorous verification of both applicants and vehicles offered as collateral.

The Problem

Business Challenge

The existing loan application process was losing potential customers. Despite good traffic volume, conversion rates from leads to approved loans remained below expectations. The company needed to understand why qualified applicants were dropping out of the process.

Key Issues Identified:

  • High drop-off rates at specific funnel stages
  • Users couldn't complete applications in one session
  • Complex documentation requirements overwhelming applicants
  • Mobile users (75-80% of traffic) struggling with desktop-first design

Research & Discovery

I conducted comprehensive user research combining qualitative and quantitative methods to understand where and why users were dropping off.

1. Empathize Phase

I conducted contextual inquiry, user observation, and created detailed personas based on real user data. I use these screens to visualize usage metrics, heatmaps, and geographic data to determine user abandonment points in the loan funnel.

Heatmap analysis revealing user behavior patterns and drop-off points in the application funnel.

Key Insights from Research

The research showed that the design was failing the majority of our users. We had very high mobile traffic (75-80%), but the design was not fully mobile-friendly. The journey was confusing, forcing users to check their email and leave the application process. Also, requiring 11 documents was too overwhelming. With these key insights from the empathize phase, I could move to the next phase to set clear priorities and solutions.

The Design Process

2. Define Phase

Using the collected research, I analyzed the data through rigorous funnel analysis, detailed information synthesis, and clusterization techniques to pinpoint the exact user pain points and uncover areas for valuable opportunity.

Streamlined Form Flow

The application form was confusing because users had to use four different screens and leave the website to check their email. I made the form much simpler. I reduced the steps from four screens to two, and I took out the email check. Now, users can finish the application faster and without leaving the page.

Comparison of bifurcated user flow versus streamlined flow eliminating email verification step.

3. Ideate & Prototype

I used card sorting to reorganize the files into just 5 logical groups, which reduced the effort for the user by 55%. I also fixed complex steps by only showing required fields. I applied error prevention by creating detailed, visual instructions for iPhone and Android users. These changes simplify the application journey and directly help to increase our user completion rates.

Error Prevention Through Education

The "technical file" requirement confused users—it's a traffic institution document that's not commonly understood.

Applied Nielsen's error prevention heuristic by creating detailed, platform-specific guidance:

  • Step-by-step visual instructions for Android and iPhone
  • Explained how to use native camera functions for high-resolution scanning
  • Placed explanatory images using proximity principle—right next to the document description

Visual instructions for iPhone and Android users.

WhatsApp Integration

Competitor analysis revealed mobile-first companies were leveraging familiar communication channels.

Integrated WhatsApp for document uploads and real-time support:

  • Added CTA button with WhatsApp link for instant call center connection
  • Integrated with CRM for seamless handoff to support team
  • Offered alternative: direct upload or scheduled callback option

WhatsApp integration for document uploads and real-time support.


4. Prototyping & Validation

I created interactive Figma prototypes and conducted unmoderated testing with Useberry to validate the design and identify any usability issues.

Mobile prototype with step-by-step guidance for document uploads and error prevention.


Final Design

The final design featured a simplified, mobile-first loan application flow with integrated WhatsApp support and consolidated documentation requirements. The new design prioritized ease of use, error prevention, and real-time assistance to enhance the overall user experience.

Final streamlined loan application flow with consolidated documentation and WhatsApp integration.

Results & Impact

The redesigned funnel exceeded growth objectives and significantly improved the user experience:

3.5% Loan Origination Growth (12 months)

Successfully scaled loan origination by 3.5% over the past 12 months while maintaining credit losses at an exceptionally low 2.2% of revenue—achieving profitability 6 months ahead of plan.

Qualitative Improvements

  • Mobile-first experience: Optimized for 75-80% of our actual user base
  • Reduced cognitive load: Simplified technical language and consolidated documentation
  • Error prevention: Step-by-step guidance reduced user mistakes
  • Instant support: WhatsApp integration enabled real-time assistance
  • Robust fallbacks: Manual entry option ensured process completion even with API failures

Reflection

This project was very complex because of all the documents, steps, and information we needed for the loan. However, this complexity taught me important things that changed how we work. My main goal was to improve the business by helping our users better. We focused on Mobile-first design because 75-80% of our users are on their phones. When most people use mobile, not fixing the design actively hurts our success. I learned that in finance, making things simple is a strategy. We must reduce friction (like simplifying documents) but still follow the rules. Also, it is much better to prevent errors (like giving step-by-step guides) than to just fix them later. Finally, building options like the API fallback and the WhatsApp support taught us that we must always plan for problems and meet our users where they already are.
