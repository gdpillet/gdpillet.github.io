CASE STUDY 01

Scaled Ibancar's loan origination by 3.5x while achieving profitability 6 months ahead of plan

Web Platform B2C Fintech

Role

Product Designer

Team

3 Developers, Marketing, CEO

Impact

  • 3.5x Loan origination was successfully scaled over the past 12 months
  • 6 Months Profitability was achieved ahead of schedule
  • 2.2% Credit losses low.

Tools

Figma, Microsoft Clarity, Google Analytics, Useberry, Jira

About Ibancar

Ibancar is a car-secured loan platform serving clients across Spain. The company connects investors with borrowers, lending capital with interest, while minimizing risk through rigorous verification of both applicants and vehicles offered as collateral.

The Challenge

Ibancar faced a critical growth bottleneck. Despite high-intent traffic, the conversion funnel was underperforming. As Lead Product Designer, I was tasked with redesigning the loan origination process to scale the business.

The challenge was to solve the 75-80% drop-off occurring within our primary mobile user segment, all while maintaining a rigorous 2.2% credit loss risk profile.

Key Issues Identified:

  • Desktop-first design failing 80% of mobile traffic.
  • High cognitive load due to 11+ document requirements.
  • Forced email verification caused users to leave the session.

Discovery & Strategic Insights

To uncover why 80% of qualified leads were dropping off, I implemented a dual research track: quantitative funnel analysis (Google Analytics/Clarity) to identify WHERE users left, and qualitative observation to understand WHY.

Heatmap and funnel analysis pinpointing the exact friction points in the mobile journey.

Key Strategic Insights:

  • While 80% of traffic was mobile, the legacy funnel was a desktop-first experience, creating immediate friction.
  • Forced email verification acted as a "kill switch" for sessions, breaking the momentum of high-intent users.
  • Requesting 11 documents upfront was perceived as a barrier, not a requirement.

The Design Strategy: Radical Simplification

1. Optimization of the Funnel Architecture

Instead of a fragmented 4-screen flow, I engineered a streamlined 2-step dynamic form.

  • Impact: Reduced screen-switching and eliminated email verification, keeping users within the conversion session.

Evolution from a bifurcated, high-friction legacy flow to a streamlined, session-persistent mobile architecture.

2. Solving the "Document Friction" (Omnichannel Support)

Recognizing that document collection was the primary abandonment point, I introduced a multi-channel upload strategy.

  • WhatsApp Integration: Implemented as a primary channel for real-time document submission and support.
  • Visual Education: Designed OS-specific guides (iOS/Android) to empower users to scan documents using native tools, reducing submission errors by 55%.

Reducing technical debt and user frustration through OS-specific scanning guides and familiar messaging channels.

3. Leveraging Familiarity: The WhatsApp Strategy:

Through a competitive benchmark of mobile-first fintech leaders, I identified that the friction of document uploads could be mitigated by leveraging familiar communication channels. I strategized the integration of WhatsApp to transform a high-friction task into a seamless interaction.

  • Omnichannel Flexibility: Implemented a one-tap CTA that allowed users to choose between direct web uploads, instant WhatsApp connection, or scheduled callbacks.
  • Operational Alignment: Designed the flow to ensure a seamless CRM handoff, ensuring that agents received documents in real-time without losing user context.

Implementing an omnichannel document strategy to meet users where they are, significantly reducing abandonment during the verification phase

Validation & Iteration

To validate the new architecture, I developed high-fidelity interactive prototypes and conducted unmoderated usability testing. This proactive phase allowed us to identify and resolve edge cases in the document scanning process, ensuring the 55% reduction in user effort was achievable.

Validating step-by-step guidance and error prevention through remote testing to ensure a frictionless mobile experience.

Business Impact & Scalability

Successfully scaled loan origination by 350% over the past 12 months while maintaining credit losses at an exceptionally low 2.2% of revenue, achieving profitability 6 months ahead of plan.

6 Months Ahead

Target profitability reached early

By prioritizing a mobile-first architecture and integrating omnichannel support via WhatsApp, we didn't just improve UX; we transformed the company's financial trajectory. Reaching profitability 6 months ahead of schedule proved that radical simplification, when backed by user research, is a powerful driver for business growth.

Reflection

"Designing for impact means aligning user needs with business profitability".

Navigating the inherent complexity of financial loans taught me that simplicity is a business strategy. With 80% of traffic on mobile, adopting a mobile-first mindset was not optional; it was critical for business survival.

Key Lessons:

  • Friction vs. Compliance: I learned to balance reducing friction with strict Fintech compliance, prioritizing proactive error prevention over simple error recovery.
  • Omnichannel Resilience: Implementing WhatsApp support reinforced that we must meet users where they already are. Building resilience into the funnel means offering fallbacks that align with real-world user behavior.
  • Design as a Profit Driver: This project proved that UX isn't just about 'look and feel'; it’s about moving the needle on profitability. Reaching our goals 6 months ahead of schedule validated that a research-backed, simplified journey is the fastest path to scale.
