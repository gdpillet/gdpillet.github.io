CASE STUDY 03

How did I design a security feature that achieved an 85% enterprise adoption rate, a 40% reduction in support tickets, and a 30% decrease in misconfigured deployments?

SaaS B2B Workflow automation

Role

Senior Product Designer

Team

2 Engineering teams(Core & UI), PM, QA, Documentation team

Core Impact

  • 85% Enterprise Adoption
  • 40% Support Ticket Reduction
  • Sales Enablement

Tools

Figma, Carbon Design System, Dovetail, Mixpanel

The Challenge

Our platform faced a critical Enterprise Sales Blocker: potential customers required control over their encryption keys (BYOEK) to meet compliance standards. However, implementing this feature carried a high risk of adding friction, as existing market solutions were notoriously difficult to configure.

As Senior Product Designer, I was tasked with designing a native BYOEK solution that would satisfy strict Security Compliance Officers while remaining usable for DevOps teams, turning a technical barrier into a seamless adoption driver.

Discovery & Strategic Insights

I led a comprehensive discovery phase, analyzing implementations from AWS and Azure and interviewing 6 enterprise customers currently in the sales pipeline.

Competitive analysis of encryption implementations across leading cloud platforms.

Key Strategic Insights:

  • Competitor analysis revealed that complex JSON configurations were the primary cause of deployment failures. Users felt intimidated by raw code interfaces.
  • Compliance officers didn't just need encryption; they needed proof. The lack of visibility into key status was a major anxiety point.
  • The person configuring the key (DevOps) wasn't the person auditing it (SecOps). The tool needed to speak both languages.

Design Strategy & Implementation

My strategy focused on "Trust through Transparency" and reducing cognitive load via Progressive Disclosure.

Inline Validation

Real-time feedback on configuration errors with specific remediation guidance

Example Templates

Pre-populated JSON configuration templates reduced manual coding errors

Progressive Disclosure

Broke the complex setup into manageable steps with clear indicators

The No-Code Configuration Strategy (The Wizard)

To solve the "Configuration Cliff", I replaced the raw JSON requirement with a two-step guided wizard.

  • Inline Validation: Designed real-time feedback loops that catch configuration errors before deployment, preventing critical failures.
  • Templates: Provided pre-populated configuration templates to reduce manual coding errors by non-experts.

Systematizing Security UI (Design System)

Recognizing that security monitoring was a long-term need, I didn't just build a page; I built a system.

  • Reusable Components: Created a dedicated library of "System Health" patterns (alert banners, connection states, diagnostic logs) now used across the entire product suite.
  • Diagnostic Visibility: Designed a log interface accessible to technical users for self-service troubleshooting, directly aiming to reduce support dependency.

Design specifications for BYOEK monitoring and status indicators.

Strategic Feature Gating

I proposed a strategic trade-off: restricting BYOEK complexity to the Enterprise Tier. This allowed us to keep the core platform simple for 90% of users while providing sophisticated, high-value controls only where needed—optimizing both UX and Business Strategy simultaneously.

Interactive prototype framework for BYOEK configuration and validation flows.

Validation & Iteration

I conducted usability testing with 5 target enterprise customers using interactive Figma prototypes.

  • Outcome: Validated that the "Wizard approach" significantly reduced anxiety compared to the JSON method. Feedback led to the addition of "Connection State Indicators" to reassure users during the migration process.

Final BYOEK prototype featuring comprehensive setup wizard and status dashboard.

Business Impact & Scale

The BYOEK implementation exceeded adoption targets and became a key differentiator in closing enterprise deals:

85% Enterprise Adoption Rate (3 months)

Within 3 months, the vast majority of eligible customers activated the feature, validating strong market demand.

40%

Reduction in support tickets: The clarity of the new dashboard and diagnostic logs allowed teams to resolve issues without contacting support.

30%

Decrease in misconfigured deployments: The validation wizard successfully prevented common setup errors, securing customer data reliability.

Reflection

Designing for enterprise security taught me that Transparency is the key to user Trust.

  • Visibility as a Feature: I realized that giving users clear visibility into their security status was just as vital as the encryption technology itself.
  • Strategic Friction: By gating complex features to the Enterprise tier, we learned that good UX sometimes means hiding complexity from users who don't need it, preserving the platform's speed for the majority.
  • Business Alignment: Validating early with decision-makers (Sales/CTOs) proved that design can directly unblock revenue streams by solving objection handling through UI.
